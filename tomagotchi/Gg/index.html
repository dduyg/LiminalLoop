magic sketchpad

What is this?

Magic Sketchpad is a little experiment in doodling with a machine instead of just by yourself. Start drawing something — a cat, a bicycle, whatever's on your mind — and the second you lift your pen, a machine learning model jumps in and tries to finish the stroke to match whatever category you've picked. Sometimes it nails it, sometimes it goes somewhere delightfully weird. Either way, it's fun to see what it comes up with.

You can keep going back and forth with it, switch categories mid-drawing, mix a few together, or just mash the retry button until the model draws something you like better.

Under the hood, it's built with magenta.js and a model called Sketch-RNN, trained on millions of doodles from Google's Quick, Draw! dataset — basically, it's seen a lot of terrible cat drawings, so yours will fit right in.
